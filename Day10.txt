Numeric Functions

1.abs() : Absolute Value of a number
2.ceil() : Rounded up
3.floor() : Roundes down
4.round() : Rounds a secific no of digits
5.mod() : Modulo Operator
6.power()/pow : Exponential
7.sqrt() : Square root of a number

SELECT ABS(-100)AS abs_val;
+---------+
| abs_val |
+---------+
|     100 |
+---------+

SELECT CEIL(12.34)AS rounded_up;

| rounded_up |
+------------+
|         13 |

SELECT FLOOR(12.34)AS rounded_down;

|           12 |
