DROP VS DELETE VS TRUNCATE
--------------------------
DELETE   :BASED ON SOME CONDITION DELETE THE RECORDS

TRUNCATE :TO DELETE ALL RECORDS PRESENT IN A TABLE

DROP     :IT DROPS THE ENTIRE TABLE AND STRUCTURE.
